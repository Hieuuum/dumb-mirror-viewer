Limitations

  1. Weaker judge. DeepSeek V4-Flash is smaller than the AuditBench paper's Claude Sonnet 4.5. Absolute scores are not comparable to paper figures; only within-experiment teacher-vs-student deltas are valid.
  2. Judge geopolitics. DeepSeek is PRC-hosted and may itself be biased on Russia / Ukraine content. Refusal rate is reported in Judge health.
  3. Quantization. Teacher served at bitsandbytes 4-bit; the paper used bf16. Expect a 5–15% drop in behavior strength relative to paper.
  4. Sample size. n=50 per metric implies roughly ±0.07 95% CI on confession rate near p=0.5. Treat small deltas with care.
